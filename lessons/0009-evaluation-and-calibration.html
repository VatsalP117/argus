Lesson 9: Evaluation And Calibration

Skill: interpret why an evaluation failure blocks wider ingestion.

Relevance scoring is a product-quality problem. A bad scorer fills the durable corpus with junk or misses the evidence the product is supposed to find. Argus uses labelled fixtures and gates to slow itself down when quality is not good enough.

The One Thing

Precision asks: "Of what we retained, how much was truly relevant?" Recall asks: "Of what was relevant, how much did we retain?" Argus also checks domain-specific precision, false-positive categories, trap leakage, and weighted recall from sampling strata.
