Lesson 1: Local-First Mental Model

Skill: explain why Argus is local-first and what tradeoff that creates.

As a fullstack developer, you may expect a service, app database, background queue, and hosted infra. Argus starts somewhere different: it is a local research pipeline. The useful product is not a live service yet; it is a reproducible way to turn bounded Reddit archive slices into evidence-backed research data.

The One Thing

Argus uses local DuckDB plus Parquet because the project is still proving research value. Local-first keeps cost low, iteration fast, artifacts inspectable, and intermediate data easy to rebuild. The tradeoff is that Argus must be very disciplined about capacity, concurrency, and reproducibility.

Repo anchors: README.md, docs/decisions/ADR-001-local-first-duckdb.md, docs/decisions/ADR-002-durable-duckdb-and-ephemeral-raw.md.

Mental Bridge

In a web app, your database is usually the source of truth. In Argus, the durable DuckDB is the local research corpus, but the remote Parquet archive remains the recoverable upstream source. That is why manifests, revisions, checksums, and checkpoints matter so much.
