Lesson 8: Candidate Scan And Relevance

Skill: explain why Argus first scans broadly, then scores and gates.

Argus cannot keep every Reddit row. It also cannot miss too many good rows. So it uses a high-recall candidate scan first, then relevance scoring and evaluation to decide what is safe to retain.

The One Thing

Candidate rules answer: "Could this row matter?" Relevance rules answer: "How much does this row matter for a domain?" The first should be broad enough not to miss useful evidence; the second should be calibrated enough not to pollute the durable corpus.

Repo anchors: configs/candidates/broad-v1.yaml, configs/relevance/deterministic-v3.yaml, cmd/scan-candidates, cmd/score-candidates.

Tiers

In deterministic-v3, scores map roughly to A/B/C/D tiers. A and B are retained candidates, C is evaluation pool, and D is discarded after metrics are recorded.
